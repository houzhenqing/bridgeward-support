Hold the bridge. Bring the last convoy home.

An offline iPhone squad-defense game by Zhenqing Hou. Escort the last evacuation convoy through 24 missions across three chapters.

## How to play

- Drag left or right to steer. Your squad shoots automatically.
- Break supply crates and choose between-wave upgrades to recruit guards and improve weapons.
- Dodge marked enemy attacks. Direct hits can remove guards; rebuild your squad with supply rewards.
- Use Pulse when enemies close in. Watch the cooldown before committing.
- Boss equipment and attack sequences change across missions. Read Enemy Intel for tactics and story.

## Common questions

**Internet or login?** Neither is required. There are no ads or in-app purchases.

**Languages?** English and Simplified Chinese follow the iPhone language settings. You can choose an app-specific language in iOS Settings when multiple languages are enabled.

**Save data?** Mission progress and stars stay on this device. There is no developer-operated cloud sync. Deleting the app removes local progress.

**Audio or vibration?** Open Settings from the mission map or pause screen to adjust music, sound effects, and haptics.

**Privacy?** Open Settings → Privacy & Support to read the bundled policy offline, or read the policy linked above.
