# Bridgeward: Pulse Siege — Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · Developer: Zhenqing Hou

## Your game stays on your device

Bridgeward: Pulse Siege works offline. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or track personal data through the game. There are no accounts, advertisements, analytics SDKs, or in-app purchases.

## Progress and preferences

Mission progress, stars, tutorial state, and audio and haptic preferences are stored locally on your device. There is no developer-operated cloud sync. Deleting the app removes its local data. Any device backups are managed by your Apple settings.

## Sharing is your choice

If you choose Share after a game, iOS opens its share sheet with a result image created on your device. Only the destination you select receives it. That service applies its own privacy policy.

## Contact and support

For help or privacy questions, email houzhenqing.colin@gmail.com. Sending an email shares your email address and the information you include. We use that correspondence only to respond and resolve your request; request deletion by email. Avoid including sensitive information.

## External services and changes

Apple handles App Store downloads and any diagnostics you choose to share under its policies. Opening the online policy uses your browser and GitHub’s services. If the game’s data practices change, this policy and its effective date will be updated.

Contact / 联系方式: houzhenqing.colin@gmail.com

# 守桥者：脉冲防线 — 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 28 日 · 开发者：Zhenqing Hou

## 游戏数据留在你的设备

守桥者：脉冲防线可离线游玩。我们不会通过游戏收集、传输、出售个人数据或进行追踪。游戏没有账号、广告、分析 SDK 或 App 内购买。

## 进度与偏好

关卡进度、星数、新手引导状态、音效及触感偏好保存在设备本地，不使用开发者运营的云同步服务。删除 App 会移除本地数据；设备备份由你的 Apple 设置管理。

## 由你决定是否分享

在结算后选择分享时，游戏会在设备上生成战绩图片并打开 iOS 分享面板。只有你选择的接收方会收到图片，该服务适用其自身的隐私政策。

## 联系与支持

如需帮助或有隐私问题，请发送邮件至 houzhenqing.colin@gmail.com。主动发送邮件会提供你的邮箱地址及邮件内容，我们仅将其用于回复和解决你的请求；可通过邮件申请删除相关通信。请勿包含敏感信息。

## 外部服务与政策更新

Apple 根据其政策处理 App Store 下载，以及你选择共享的诊断信息。打开在线政策会使用浏览器及 GitHub 服务。如果游戏的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本政策及生效日期。

Contact / 联系方式: houzhenqing.colin@gmail.com
